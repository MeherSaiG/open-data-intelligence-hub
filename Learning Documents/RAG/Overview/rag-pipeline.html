The RAG pipeline, stage by stage

Following one question through the system: “Can I carry over unused leave if I’m on the Chennai payroll?”

  1. Chunk and tag

    Offline, whenever documents change

    Split the policy into sections and attach metadata so chunks can be filtered later.

    { "id": "IN-2026",
  "text": "Chennai payroll staff: up to 10 days of earned leave roll forward...",
  "region": "IN-Chennai", "year": 2026, "status": "current" }

  2. Embed and index

    Offline

    An embedding model turns each chunk into a vector. Similar meaning gives nearby vectors. These numbers are illustrative.

    IN-2026 → [0.12, -0.48, 0.33, ...]  (hundreds to thousands of dimensions)

  3. Retrieve

    At question time

    Embed the question, search the index, apply metadata filters, rerank, and keep what passes a relevance threshold.

    filter: status = current, region in (global, IN-Chennai)
result: IN-2026 (rank 1), G-2026 (rank 2)

  4. Augment

    At question time

    Put the retrieved chunks and the question in one prompt, with an instruction to stay within the context.

    Answer using ONLY the context. Cite chunk IDs.
[IN-2026] Chennai payroll staff: up to 10 days...
[G-2026]  Employees may carry over up to 3 days...
Question: Can I carry over unused leave...

  5. Generate

    At question time

    The LLM writes the answer from the supplied context and cites it.

    “Yes. On the Chennai payroll up to 10 days roll forward, and the remainder
is encashed in March [IN-2026].”
Debugging tip: check stage 3 first. If the right chunk is missing from the prompt, fix retrieval (metadata, hybrid search, reranking) before touching the prompt or the model. In 2026 setups, an agent may repeat stages 3 to 4 several times before generating.
